About Our Yoga Community

Meet Our Instructors

Our compassionate teaching team is dedicated to supporting your unique wellness journey. Every instructor has extensive training in modifying postures for diverse body types, age brackets, and physical limitations.

Accessibility Accommodations

Inclusivity sits at the heart of our community values. Our physical studio features step-free ramp entrances, extra-wide hallways, and an open layout to accommodate mobility aids comfortably. We provide a full assortment of supportive props, including chairs, extra-thick mats, straps, and foam blocks, ensuring that every practice is accessible to everyone.

Studio Policies

To preserve our peaceful atmosphere, we request that all guests turn off cell phones before entering the practice room. Please arrive ten minutes prior to class time to settle in comfortably. Mats are always provided at no additional charge if you do not bring your own.
