Our Class Offerings & Services

Group Class Styles

  • Gentle Yoga: A slow-paced session focusing on fundamental alignments, stretching, and deep breathing. Perfect for older adults and absolute beginners.
  • Vinyasa Flow: A dynamic practice that coordinates movement with breath, structured to help busy professionals build strength and release accumulated stress.
  • Restorative Yoga: A deeply soothing practice using supportive props to hold restful postures, allowing the body and mind to completely unwind.

Private Yoga Sessions

For individuals seeking specialized guidance, our private sessions offer customized sequences tailored to your personal mobility goals, injuries, or scheduling constraints. These sessions provide an excellent, low-pressure entry point for newcomers anxious about entering a group class setting.
